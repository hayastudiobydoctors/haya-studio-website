About Haya Studio

Haya Studio by Doctors was founded by Dr. Afsha Shaikh and Dr. Sharique Shaikh, a husband-and-wife team bringing together creativity, care, and vision. With one founder equipped with cosmetology expertise and the other with management and MBA insight, Haya Studio was created to build a modern, trusted, and customer-focused modest fashion brand.

As Kurla East continues to grow rapidly, many women still travel to distant markets to find quality abayas, burkhas, and modest fashion choices. Haya Studio was born with a simple idea — to make elegant modest fashion easily accessible with quality, comfort, and trust for women in every neighborhood.

Our collections include stylish abayas, graceful burkhas, burkha accessories, latest Pakistani dresses, and evolving women’s fashion essentials. As we grow, we aim to expand into wider categories including accessories, children’s clothing, and family lifestyle essentials.

What makes Haya Studio special is our personal approach. We carefully select styles that combine elegance, practicality, and current trends instead of offering random mass-market stock. Every customer is treated with attention, respect, and prompt service.

We are beginning locally for the families and women of Kurla East and nearby areas, with a larger dream of serving customers across Mumbai and beyond.

Our long-term vision is bold — to grow Haya Studio into one of the most trusted and recognized fashion destinations in Kurla East, with a deluxe physical studio known for style, quality, and customer satisfaction.
